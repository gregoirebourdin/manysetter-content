Speed to lead: a Harvard study found that answering a lead within an hour makes a real conversation nearly 7x more likely. Here's what it means for your Instagram DMs.

Harvard Business Review looked at 1.25 million sales leads. Firms that answered within an hour were nearly 7 times more likely to get a real conversation than firms that waited one more hour, and more than 60 times more likely than firms that waited a day.

They also sent a fake lead to 2,241 companies. 23% never answered.

How long does your average DM wait? Comment your number, no judgment.

Source: Oldroyd, McElheran and Elkington, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads", Harvard Business Review, March 2011.
