👇 READ BELOW: the DM script I made to book calls without sounding scripted.

1. Answer their question first, price included. Dodging the price feels shady.

2. Ask one question at a time, never two in the same message.

3. Check the fit before the calendar: timing and budget.

4. Offer two times instead of a link, and when they say "I'll think about it", ask which part they're unsure about.
