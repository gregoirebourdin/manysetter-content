The character: Seth, a man around 30 years old with an athletic, muscular fitness-coach build: broad shoulders, defined chest, muscular arms and forearms, lean, not a bodybuilder. Warm light-brown skin with real, unretouched detail: visible pores, light freckles across his nose and cheekbones, a small mole on his left cheekbone, a thin pale scar through the outer end of his right eyebrow, faint smile lines, a slightly asymmetric smile. No headphones. Dark straight-leg jeans and white sneakers.

Layout, all the same person with identical face, facial details, platinum buzz cut, dark beard, neck tattoo, skin tone, white t-shirt and orange watch:
1. head and shoulders, facing camera, neutral friendly expression
2. head and shoulders, three-quarter view left
3. head and shoulders, profile right
4. full body standing, facing camera, relaxed pose, arms slightly away from the body so the orange watch and the muscular arms are visible
5. a row of 4 small close-up expressions: laughing, raised eyebrow, skeptical squint, warm smile

Vérifier avant de valider : il ne fait pas « banal » (retour du 10/10), le visage et ses détails sont identiques dans les 5 vues, le tatouage avion en papier est au même endroit, la montre est orange, le buzz cut est bien platine et très court partout, la barbe courte reste bien foncée (le contraste est sa signature), un petit anneau argent à chaque oreille, aucun casque, les mains ont cinq doigts, aucun texte n'apparaît.
