## Facts and sources (checked, `00_STRATEGIE/sources/banque-2.md`, idea 17)

- Line 2, the build: in Manychat, an External Request (Dev Tools) in the Default Reply sends the lead's message to a Make
  Custom webhook (K8, K4); a ChatGPT "Generate a response" module writes the reply with your instructions (K6); Webhook
  Response sends it back and Manychat posts it from a custom field (K4, K8). Each brick is documented, the assembly is
  ours and was **not tested**: the reel shows it as what the coach built from the docs, never as a guaranteed tutorial.
  The card names the real modules; no Manychat plan name anywhere.
- Lines 3 and 4: each lead message fires the Default Reply, so its own request and its own run: three messages, three
  replies, unless you build a wait yourself (Make's Sleep stops at 300 s, K9). Level: deduction (banque-2, "non
  trouvé"), the same point as the carousel's slide 6. The coach finds it out loud; Setty never states it as a measured
  fact. mc22 is about Manychat's own AI: only the feeling, written as "my AI", never attributed to Manychat.
- Lines 5 to 7: the "Conversation ID" field exists in the OpenAI module (K6); it does not wire itself, you store it per
  lead in a Manychat custom field and send it back each time ("memory doesn't wire itself", never "ChatGPT has no
  memory"). Fit questions, booking, follow-ups: nothing in these modules does it (banque-2 table, safe line "all yours
  to build and maintain").
- Line 9: Make bills in credits, most modules one credit per run (K1); with your own OpenAI connection, you pay OpenAI
  for every token (K2). Never "Make charges per operation".
- Line 10: rd67, "I'm not a developer (…) another thing to maintain" (tool names left out).
- Line 11: it waits until the lead has finished typing (P4, at least 30 s after the last message, never "instant"),
  remembers what the lead said (P7), one question at a time (P6), books the call on Cal.com, Calendly (paid) or iClosed,
  else sends the link (P9, P9b: the card says "books the call", no calendar named), up to 3 follow-ups inside 24 h (P8).
  "The model isn't the setter": the carousel's line.
- Never: "Make has an Instagram DM module" (its Instagram app has none, K7), a Manychat plan name, "replies instantly",
  a Make or OpenAI logo as an endorsement. The caption keeps "Not affiliated with Make, OpenAI, Manychat or Meta."
