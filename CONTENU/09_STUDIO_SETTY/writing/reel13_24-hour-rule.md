## Facts and sources (all from `00_STRATEGIE/sources/banque-2.md`, idea 16, Meta's and Manychat's docs read on 20/10/2026)

- Line 3 (R1): when a lead messages you on Instagram or Messenger, a 24-hour window opens; inside it you can reply
  freely, even with an offer. Setty never says "always": after a click-to-message ad the window can last up to 7 days
  (R13, A5), so the line says what Meta gives you when a lead DMs you, nothing more.
- Line 5 (R3, R10): after 24 hours, automated messages are not allowed under Meta's policy, and Manychat won't send the
  scheduled ones once the 24 hours are up.
- Line 7 (R4, R6): for 7 days after the lead's message, a person can still reply by hand (Meta's Human Agent tag, for
  people, never automated), and message tags can't carry promotions. "March is a graveyard": his March contacts are
  past the 24 hours and past the 7 days. Nothing says "you can never message them again" (do-not-write list n° 2):
  line 11 says the window opens again when they write.
- Line 9 (R9): Manychat's help page says the rules are set by Meta. Nothing against Manychat (verbatims rule 3).
- Line 11 (R2): every new message from the lead restarts the 24 hours. No "find an excuse to message at 23 h"
  (do-not-write list n° 24, cl047 not used): the chat goes on as long as the lead writes.
- Line 13 (P4, P8, R12): the setter waits until the lead has finished typing (at least 30 s after the last message,
  never "instant"), then sends up to 3 follow-ups between 1 and 23 hours after the lead's last message, so every one
  lands inside the 24-hour window.
- The hook "nobody buys": the reader's situation (mc17: 13,000 contacts and no idea how to monetize them). The reel
  explains why you can't just message them all, then where the sale happens: while the lead is talking.
- No message tag, no Human Agent trick, no date for the end of the old tags (R7), nothing that promises to avoid a ban
  (verbatims rule 5). The caption keeps "ManySetter is not affiliated with or endorsed by Manychat or Meta."
- The voice script says "ManyChat" (for the TTS), the screen always reads "Manychat".
