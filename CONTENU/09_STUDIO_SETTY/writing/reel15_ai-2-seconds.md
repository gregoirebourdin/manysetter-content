# reel15, "AI in 2 seconds" (v1, dialogue, for approval)

Idea 18 of the editorial line (`00_STRATEGIE/ligne-edito.md`, bank n° 2), paired with the carousel
`261020_CAR_S2_AV2_ai-2-seconds` (same topic, same day). A real conversation (CLAUDE.md § 2b): the coach is proud that his
AI auto-reply answers every DM in two seconds, and he is upgrading his servers to make it one; Setty shows him, message
by message, what two seconds does to a lead who is still typing, then the product comes up because the coach asks.
Structure of the editorial line: false belief ("speed is the flex") → problems (one thought in three messages, a reply
to each one, the booking link before the end of the sentence, the goal asked again) → consequences (a form, not a chat;
"my AI answers instantly" is the complaint he gets) → solution (wait until they are done, one reply, one question) →
benefits (it remembers, never asks twice) → CTA.

Never sells speed (lot rule, `sources/banque-2.md` « À ne pas écrire » n° 1): ManySetter is never "fast", "instant" or
"in seconds". The product line says what it does instead: it waits until the lead is done typing. mc22 is about
Manychat's own AI: the coach says "my AI", Manychat is never blamed (règle 3 des verbatims, À ne pas écrire n° 20).

black flight case (orange latches), phone in hand, a crash cart with a little monitor next to him; his own rack behind
him wears a label screen "AUTO-REPLY · 2.0 s". Setty sits on a server pulled out of its rack on its rails, on the other
side. Cool blues and steel everywhere: no orange behind Setty, the only warm light is the key on their faces. The joke of
the set: he went to a data center to make his bot one second faster (the punchline).
