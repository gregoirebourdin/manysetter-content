## Facts and sources (checked)

- Hook title and the whole line of argument: the carousel `261019_CAR_S2_AV2_calendly-no-shows`, validated by the
  founder on 20/10 (slides 1 to 9). The 90 % is the reader's situation, not a statistic.
- Lines 2 and 7: the setup everyone uses, the Calendly link in the auto DM and the bio (ligne-edito idea 8, carousel
  slide 2); the link asks nothing (slide 3).
- Line 9: "a free call costs them nothing, so skipping it costs them nothing too" (carousel slide 4).
- Lines 11 and 13: the setter qualifies one question at a time, only gives the calendar to the right leads, and gives
  the others a useful answer (the site's demo example: a free guide instead of a call). Source: `brief-de-marque.md` § 2.
- Line 15: with Cal.com, iClosed or a **paid** Calendly plan, the setter offers 2 or 3 times in the lead's time zone,
  confirms the email and books the call with a summary of the lead in the event; otherwise it sends the booking link.
  Source: `brief-de-marque.md` § 2 (the carousel's `post.json` source). Setty says "Paid Calendly?" so the line stays true.
- No promise: nothing says the no-shows stop. The caption's "The catch" says it: no tool can stop every no-show, what
  changes is who gets your calendar.
- Demo values, not results: Tyler, Kayla, 15:02, "third no-show this week" are the scene's fiction (UI cards and the
  coach's own week), never a ManySetter number.
- Zoom and Calendly are named as the coach's tools (logos only through `st.app`, official Simple Icons glyphs); no
  competitor of ManySetter is named; "Manychat" on screen (the voice script says "ManyChat" for the TTS).
